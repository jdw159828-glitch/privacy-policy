Common questions

Are these real exam questions?
No, and the app does not claim to be. Every question and explanation was written for this app. They cover film and sensor placement, exposure factors, image quality and errors, radiation physics and protection, and infection control at the chair. They are not retired examination items, and the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any dental board, certification body or examination.
Is the mrem reading a real dose?
No. It is a scoring scale inside the app. A retake adds twelve to the reading and three diagnostic films in a row take ten off, so the number tracks how often you had to shoot again. It is not a measurement of anything, and it says nothing about the exposure you or a patient receives. Your real dose comes from your badge service and your state's rules.
Why does the reading stay up after I close the app?
Because a badge is read over a period, not per session. The reading carries over until you work it down with clean films. If you want to start fresh, Settings has "Start a new badge period", which zeroes the reading and keeps the films you have already shot.
What is a mount?
Eighteen films, the size of a full-mouth series. The questions are dealt out across mounts so a sitting has a natural end, and you can also open any single mount on its own.
How much is free?
Twenty-seven films on your first day and twelve a day after that, across all three sets, with no account and no sign-in. A subscription removes the daily limit.
How do I cancel?
Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. The app cannot cancel a subscription for you — Apple does not allow it. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
I subscribed but the app still shows a daily limit
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore. If you subscribed with a different Apple Account, sign in to that account first. If it still does not appear, email the address above and include the Apple Account email you paid with.
Did I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move between devices.
I think a question is wrong
Please send the set name, the film number shown above the question, and what you think the answer should be. Questions get corrected in the next update.
