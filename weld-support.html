Common questions

Are these real exam questions?
No, and the app does not claim to be. Every question and explanation was written for this app. They cover welding processes and metallurgy, non-destructive examination, procedure and welder qualification, and the construction of pipelines and welded storage tanks. They are not retired examination items, no published standard is reproduced, and the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any standards body, certification programme or examination provider.
Why does a question name a standard?
For the same reason a textbook does — to say which body of technical material the question is about. A question about girth welds has to be able to say so. Naming a published standard is not a claim of any connection to the organisation that publishes it.
Why does a cut-out take two correct answers to clear?
Because that is the field. You cut out the joint, re-weld it and re-shoot it, and one film does not settle it. If you miss in between, the count starts over. The repair list is deliberately the most expensive list in the app: it holds exactly the material you have already shown you do not know.
What is "line walked"?
Accepted joints counted as feet of pipe at forty feet a joint — the standard length of a joint of line pipe. It is the same number as accepted joints, said the way the work is measured on site.
How much is free?
Twenty-five joints on your first day and ten a day after that, across all three lines, with no account and no sign-in. A subscription removes the daily limit.
How do I cancel?
Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. The app cannot cancel a subscription for you — Apple does not allow it. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
I subscribed but the app still shows a daily limit
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore. If you subscribed with a different Apple Account, sign in to that account first. If it still does not appear, email the address above and include the Apple Account email you paid with.
Did I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move between devices.
I think a question is wrong
Please send the line name, the weld number shown above the question, and what you think the call should be. Questions get corrected in the next update.

Privacy
