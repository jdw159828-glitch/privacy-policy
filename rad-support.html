Common questions

Are these real exam questions?
No, and the app does not claim to be. Every question and explanation was written for this app. They cover radiation physics and protection, exposure factors and image production, image evaluation and quality control, equipment, spine positioning, and patient care and contrast. They are not retired examination items, and the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any registry, professional society, school or examination.
Why is a row I got right "on hold"?
Because a row above it on the same sheet is wrong. A technique chart is derived downward — if the baseline is wrong, the rows under it cannot be relied on, even when the reasoning is right. Fix the bad row and every held row below it locks at the same moment.
How do I find the row that is holding things up?
Open the fix list. It contains exactly the wrong rows, in order, and fixing one shows you how many rows it freed.
Can I practise without the sheet rule?
Yes. "Mixed rows" is single questions with no sheet and no hold state. The rule applies to "Next sheet" and "One sheet".
Is the technique sheet a real chart?
No. The sheets, the hold state and the lock state are a scoring device inside this app, built to teach one habit: find the upstream error instead of patching the rows under it. Do not expose to anything in here — your department's chart is the one that counts.
How much is free?
Thirty rows on your first day and twelve a day after that, across all six sets, with no account and no sign-in. A subscription removes the daily limit.
How do I cancel?
Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. The app cannot cancel a subscription for you — Apple does not allow it. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
I subscribed but the app still shows a daily limit
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore. If you subscribed with a different Apple Account, sign in to that account first. If it still does not appear, email the address above and include the Apple Account email you paid with.
Did I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move between devices.
I think a question is wrong
Please send the set name, the sheet and row number shown above the question, and what you think the answer should be. Questions get corrected in the next update.
