Common questions

Does one purchase cover all three sets?
Yes. A single subscription unlocks all three sets. The 12 free questions a day (30 on your first day) are also shared across all three, and each set keeps its own progress, so working one does not disturb the others.
What is included?
Surgical Technology (349 questions on sterile technique, instruments and procedures), Orthopedic Assisting (194 on bone, joints, hardware and positioning), and Operating Room Practice (119 on room setup, counts, specimens and safety).
What does the subscription open?
It removes the daily limit and opens the timed full case and read-through mode (answers and explanations laid open, unscored). The everyday practice modes work on the free tier, within the daily allowance.
Where is my progress stored?
On your device only. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
I paid but the app still shows the free limit.
Open Subscribe and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.
How do I cancel?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by this app. Open Settings in the app, tap your plan, then Manage in Apple settings — that opens Apple's own subscription panel. You can also get there from the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.
What happens if I switch plans?
Apple handles it. Moving to a longer plan takes effect immediately and Apple credits back the unused part of the current period. Moving to a shorter plan takes effect when the current period ends — nothing is charged before then, and the app keeps showing your current plan until the switch happens.
Does it work without a network?
Yes. The questions, explanations and fonts are all inside the app. Nothing is fetched while you study.
I think an answer is wrong.
Please email the set name, the question number and what you believe the correct answer is. Corrections are taken seriously and shipped in the next update.
