Common questions

Are these real exam questions?
No, and the app does not claim to be. Every question and explanation was written for this app. They cover theory and calculations, climbing and equipment, transformers and connections, three-phase systems, grounding and protection, switching, and rigging. They are not retired examination items, and the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any union, apprenticeship committee, training provider or examination.
Why does the line stop even though I got later spans right?
Because a line is energized from the substation outward, and it stops at the first open point. Spans past an open point can be made up perfectly and they still sit dark. Close the nearest open point and everything behind it lights up at once — the app tells you how many spans just came on.
What is the fastest way to move the line?
Open the "Open points" list. It is sorted nearest the substation first, which is the order that actually moves the energized distance. Working the far end first feels productive and moves nothing.
What is a span worth?
Three hundred feet, so roughly eighteen spans to the mile. The mileage is just the span count in units that read like a day's work.
Is any of this a work method?
No. The line, the poles and the energized distance are a scoring device inside the app. Clearances, switching, grounding and rigging on the job follow your employer's rules, your switching orders and your foreman — not a practice app.
How much is free?
Thirty-two spans on your first day and twelve a day after that, across all six sets, with no account and no sign-in. A subscription removes the daily limit.
How do I cancel?
Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. The app cannot cancel a subscription for you — Apple does not allow it. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
I subscribed but the app still shows a daily limit
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore. If you subscribed with a different Apple Account, sign in to that account first. If it still does not appear, email the address above and include the Apple Account email you paid with.
Did I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move between devices.
I think a question is wrong
Please send the line name, the span number shown above the question, and what you think the answer should be. Questions get corrected in the next update.

Privacy
