Common questions

What is in the app?
Seven trade tracks — line work, rigging and cranes, pest control, quality engineering, remote pilot, welding inspection and boating safety — in twenty-five sets, 6,586 practice steps, each with a written explanation. One subscription covers every track; nothing is sold separately and there is no companion app.
Are these real exam questions?
No, and the app does not claim to be. Every question and explanation was written for this app. They are not retired examination items, and the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any union, apprenticeship committee, licensing board, training provider or examination.
Why did everything past my mistake go dark?
Because the run is scored from the start, not question by question. Only the unbroken stretch from step one counts as cleared. A wrong answer becomes an open point, and every step past it stays dark — including ones you had already got right.
So I lost those steps?
No. They are held, not lost. Close the open point and the whole stretch behind it clears in one go — sometimes thirty steps at once. Nothing fails and nothing is taken away; the only number that moves is how far the run is cleared.
Why score it that way?
Because field work is sequential. A line walk, a lift plan, an inspection route, an audit chain, a preflight check, a weld map, a pre-departure check: one unresolved item upstream and nothing downstream is signed off. A percentage treats every question as independent, which is the one thing these jobs are not.
Why are these seven trades in the same app?
Because they are worked the same way — in order, with a sign-off at each step — and because people hold more than one ticket. A journeyman adds a rigging card; a pest applicator adds a drone rating for roof inspections; an inspector works welds and audits. Seven tracks under one subscription costs less than seven apps.
Can I practise without the run rule?
Yes. "Mixed steps" is every step shuffled, with no run to clear and no open points. "Open points" shows only the steps holding the run back, nearest first. The run rule applies to "Walk the run" and "One section".
What is a section?
Twenty-five steps. The bar under each section number shows how much of it is signed off, so you can go straight to the part of a set you are weakest on.
Why can't I start a journeyman exam?
The timed exams are part of a subscription. Practice is not — every one of the 6,586 steps is reachable for free, a few a day. We made it this way because a 50-step timed exam cannot be done a few steps at a time: you would be stopped halfway with the clock running, which is worse than not starting it. The pass mark is 70%.
Are the boating sets specific to my state?
Partly. Rules of the road and navigation are general; the state sets are labelled by state, currently Colorado and Alabama. Boating education requirements are set by each state and they change — check your own state's current rule before you rely on it.
Is any of this a work procedure?
No. The run, the open point and the cleared count are a scoring device invented for this app. Nothing in it is a work method. Follow your employer's procedures, the manufacturer's instructions, the load chart in front of you and your jurisdiction's rules.
How much is free?
Thirty-two steps on your first day and twelve a day after that, across all twenty-five sets, with no account and no sign-in. A subscription removes the daily limit and opens the timed exams.
How do I cancel?
Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. The app cannot cancel a subscription for you — Apple does not allow it. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
I subscribed but the app still shows a daily limit
Open Settings inside the app (the gear, top right) and tap Restore. If you subscribed with a different Apple Account, sign in to that account first. If it still does not appear, email the address above and include the Apple Account email you paid with.
Did I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move between devices.
I think a question is wrong
Please send the track, the set name, and the step number shown above the question, along with what you think the answer should be. Questions get corrected in the next update.

Privacy

The app collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising identifier and no network requests at all — the questions, the fonts and the whole interface ship inside the app. See the privacy policy.
