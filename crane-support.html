Common questions

Are these real exam questions?
No, and the app does not claim to be. Every question and explanation was written for this app. They cover crane types and components, hydraulics and controls, inspections, slings and hitches, load weight, rigging plans, electrical clearances, ground conditions and the paperwork a lift needs. They are not retired examination items, and the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any certifying body, training provider or examination.
Are the radius rows a real load chart?
No. The ten rows are a way of laying the questions out and of pricing a mistake — a miss at ten feet costs six percent of your stability, the same miss at seventy costs twenty, which is the one thing about a load chart worth carrying in your head. The tonnages shown are not any machine's rated capacities. On the job, lift to the chart in the cab.
Why did my shift stop?
Stability ran out. Each shift starts at 100 percent; misses take it away by radius and correct calls give three back. At zero the crane tips and the shift ends where it stands. The lifts you made still count on the chart — only the shift ends.
Why are there no questions about a specific machine?
Because they were removed. Forty-seven questions asked about one manufacturer's hydraulic circuits, relief-valve pressures, part locations and model numbers. Those answers cannot be checked, they name a third party's product, and they are wrong on the next machine you climb into. Three more questions depended on a picture the app does not have.
How much is free?
Twenty-eight lifts on your first day and eleven a day after that, across all four sets, with no account and no sign-in. A subscription removes the daily limit.
How do I cancel?
Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. The app cannot cancel a subscription for you — Apple does not allow it. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
I subscribed but the app still shows a daily limit
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore. If you subscribed with a different Apple Account, sign in to that account first. If it still does not appear, email the address above and include the Apple Account email you paid with.
Did I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move between devices.
I think a question is wrong
Please send the set name, the lift number shown above the question, and what you think the call should be. Questions get corrected in the next update.

Privacy
