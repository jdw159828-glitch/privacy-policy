Common questions

Are these real exam questions?
No, and the app does not claim to be. Every question and explanation was written for this app. They cover clinical procedure and patient care, vitals and specimens, anatomy and terminology, privacy and safety, scheduling, coding and records. They are not retired examination items, and the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any certifying body, registry or examination.
Why does the clock move when I answer?
Because the cost of a wrong answer here is time. A step you get right takes two minutes of clinic time; a step you get wrong takes five, since you had to go back. A patient is booked every fifteen minutes and the waiting room has three chairs, so working at five minutes a step fills the room and someone eventually leaves. Nothing stops you and no session fails — the app just keeps the tally.
Is the schedule realistic?
It is a scoring device, not a model of a real clinic. Two and five minutes, fifteen minutes between patients and three chairs were chosen so that a careful pace keeps the room clear and a careless one does not. Your own clinic's numbers are its own.
What is "minutes per patient"?
Total clinic minutes divided by patients handed off. It is the number the app leads with because it is the one that captures both accuracy and pace. Ten minutes is a perfect patient; twenty-five means every step was redone.
Why do the questions say "medical assistant" and not a credential?
Because credential abbreviations are the marks of private certifying organisations. Where a question used one as a job title, it was rewritten to "medical assistant" — the question and the answer are unchanged.
How much is free?
Thirty steps on your first day and ten a day after that, across all six sets, with no account and no sign-in. A subscription removes the daily limit.
How do I cancel?
Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. The app cannot cancel a subscription for you — Apple does not allow it. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
I subscribed but the app still shows a daily limit
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore. If you subscribed with a different Apple Account, sign in to that account first. If it still does not appear, email the address above and include the Apple Account email you paid with.
Did I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move between devices.
I think a question is wrong
Please send the set name, the patient and step number shown above the question, and what you think the answer should be. Questions get corrected in the next update.

Privacy

The app collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising identifier and no network requests at all — the questions, the fonts and the whole interface ship inside the app. See the privacy policy.
