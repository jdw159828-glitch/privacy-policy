Common questions

What is in the app?
Ten allied health tracks — medical assisting, radiography, imaging modalities, opticianry, phlebotomy, pharmacy technician, EKG and cardiac, audiology, surgical technology and dialysis. Forty sets in all, 10,604 practice questions, each with a written explanation. One subscription covers every track; nothing is sold separately and there is no companion app.
Are these real exam questions?
No, and the app does not claim to be. Every question and explanation was written for this app. They are not retired examination items, and the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any certifying body, registry, school or examination.
Why did the clock jump three minutes?
Because a step you get wrong has to be redone. A correct step takes two minutes, a wrong one takes five. That is the whole cost of a mistake — nothing fails and nothing is taken away.
Someone left the clinic. What happened?
Patients arrive every fifteen minutes and the waiting room holds three. If you are slow enough that all three seats are full when the next person arrives, that person does not stay. Answer at a steady pace and it never happens.
What are the five steps?
Call in and verify, check the order, history and meds, prep and assist, chart and hand off. They are the same five in every track, because the order, the history and the handoff exist whether you are drawing blood, filling a script, running a dialysis machine or taking a radiograph.
Can I practise without the clock?
Yes. "Mixed steps" is single questions with no patient and no clock, and the follow-up list works the same way. The clock applies to "Next patient", "One session" and the credential exam.
Is any of this clinical advice?
No. Scope of practice, supervision and documentation rules are set by your state and your employer, and they change. The questions are practice material; work to your own protocols and your supervisor's direction.
How much is free?
Thirty steps on your first day and ten a day after that, across all forty sets, with no account and no sign-in. A subscription removes the daily limit and opens the timed credential exams.
Why can't I start a credential exam?
The timed exams are part of a subscription. Practice is not — every one of the 10,604 questions is reachable for free, a few steps a day. The subscription buys volume and the timed mock exam. We made it this way because a 50-step timed exam cannot be done a few steps at a time: you would be stopped halfway with the clock running, which is worse than not starting it.
How do I cancel?
Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. The app cannot cancel a subscription for you — Apple does not allow it. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
I subscribed but the app still shows a daily limit
Open Settings inside the app (the gear, top right) and tap Restore. If you subscribed with a different Apple Account, sign in to that account first. If it still does not appear, email the address above and include the Apple Account email you paid with.
Did I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move between devices.
I think a question is wrong
Please send the track, the set name, and the patient and step number shown above the question, along with what you think the answer should be. Questions get corrected in the next update.

Privacy
