Common questions

Are these real exam questions?
No, and the app does not claim to be. Every question and explanation was written for this app. They cover anatomy and site selection, order of draw, tubes and additives, labeling and specimen rejection, lab values, special patients, non-blood specimens and the departments that run the tests. They are not retired examination items, and the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any certification body, training provider or examination.
Why did one wrong answer put the next tube at risk?
Because that is what order of draw is about: what is in one tube can carry over into the next one. A spoilt tube flags the tube after it. Answer the flagged one correctly and you caught it. Miss it as well and the rack is redrawn from its first tube — at most ten questions, and only in the rack modes.
Can I practise without the rack rule?
Yes. "Mixed draw" and the "Redraw list" are not racks, so a miss there just puts the tube on the redraw list. The rack rule applies to "Next rack" and "One rack".
Are the cap colours the real order of draw?
They follow the standard clinical order — blood culture, light blue, red, gold, green, lavender, gray, pink. The colours are there to keep the order in front of you while you work. Your own facility's procedure is what governs at the chair.
How much is free?
Thirty tubes on your first day and ten a day after that, across all five sets, with no account and no sign-in. A subscription removes the daily limit.
How do I cancel?
Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. The app cannot cancel a subscription for you — Apple does not allow it. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
I subscribed but the app still shows a daily limit
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore. If you subscribed with a different Apple Account, sign in to that account first. If it still does not appear, email the address above and include the Apple Account email you paid with.
Did I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move between devices.
I think a question is wrong
Please send the set name, the tube number shown above the question, and what you think the answer should be. Questions get corrected in the next update.
