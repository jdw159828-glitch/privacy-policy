Common questions

Are these real exam questions?
No, and the app does not claim to be. Every question and explanation was written for this app. They are concept questions — coding rules, anatomy and terminology, documentation and compliance — rather than the code-book lookups that make up much of a real certification exam. Only a small share ask you to pick a code number. Use this to drill what you have to know before you open a manual, not as a substitute for practising in the current code books.
Does one purchase cover all eleven queues?
Yes. A single subscription unlocks all eleven. The 12 free claims a day (30 on your first day) are also shared across all of them, and each queue keeps its own progress and its own clean claim rate, so working one does not disturb the others.
What is included?
Inpatient Coding (1,010 items), Compliance and Auditing (578), Coding Essentials (491), Anesthesia Coding (258), Outpatient Coding (257), Practice Management (254), Risk Adjustment (250), Medical Fundamentals (240), Coding Guidelines (226), Hospital Coding (214) and Physician Coding (204) — 3,982 questions in all, each with a written explanation.
What does the subscription open?
It removes the daily limit and opens the scored proficiency test and read-through mode (answers and explanations laid open, unscored). Down the queue, Mixed queue, Denials and One batch all work on the free tier, within the daily allowance.
What is a denial?
A claim you answered wrongly. It goes into the Denials worklist and stays there until you answer it correctly, which is what the Denials mode runs through. Your clean claim rate is the share of worked claims that posted on the first pass.
Where is my progress stored?
On your device only. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
I paid but the app still shows the free limit.
Open Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.
How do I cancel?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by this app. Open Settings in the app, tap your plan, then Manage in Apple settings — that opens Apple's own subscription panel. You can also get there from the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep access until the end of the period you have paid for.
What happens if I switch plans?
Apple handles it. Moving to a longer plan takes effect immediately and Apple credits back the unused part of the current period. Moving to a shorter plan takes effect when the current period ends — nothing is charged before then, and the app keeps showing your current plan until the switch happens.
Does it work without a network?
Yes. The questions, explanations and fonts are all inside the app. Nothing is fetched while you work.
I think an answer is wrong.
Please email the queue name, the claim number and what you believe the correct answer is — a citation to the official guidelines helps most. Corrections are taken seriously and shipped in the next update.
