Common questions

Does one subscription cover all 23 banks?
Yes. A single subscription unlocks every bank in the app. There is no per-bank purchase. The free allowance is shared too: 25 questions on your first day, then ten a day, counted across all 23 banks rather than per bank.
Which banks are included?
Full-length exams (7): Full Exam A, Full Exam B, Basic Certification, Skills Review, Midterm Review, Practice Set C, Practice Set D. Clinical core (6): Airway & Breathing, Soft-Tissue & Bleeding, Medical Conditions, OB & Neonatal, Pharmacology, Anatomy & Terminology. Operations & systems (5): Scene Operations, Terrorism & Disaster, EMS Systems & Wellness, EMS Fundamentals, Legal & Ethical. Module drills (5): Preparatory, Airway, Assessment, Medical, Trauma & Ops. Each bank keeps its own progress, so working one does not disturb the others.
Are these real exam questions?
No. They are practice questions written to the topics the certification exams cover — assessment, airway, cardiology, trauma, medical emergencies, operations, and the legal and ethical material. No question is taken from any actual examination, and nothing here is a transcript of one.
Does it cover my state's exam?
The content follows the national EMS education standards that entry-level EMT examinations are built on, and most of that is the same everywhere — airway management, shock, cardiac arrest and trauma care do not change at a state line. What each state sets for itself is licensure eligibility, scope of practice, protocols and drug formularies, and those are not in the app. Your local protocols always win. Confirm licensing rules with your own state EMS office.
What does a subscription open?
It removes the daily limit of ten questions and opens mock exams (timed and scored against the pass mark) and read-through (answers and explanations laid open, page by page, unscored). Practice in order, random draw and redo missed all work on the free tier, within the daily ten.
Where is my progress stored?
On your device only. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
I paid but the app still shows the free limit.
Open Subscribe and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.
How do I cancel a subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by this app. Open Settings in the app and tap Subscription, then Cancel subscription — that opens Apple's own subscription panel. You can also get there from the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the current period and you keep access until then.
Does it work without a network?
Yes. The questions, explanations and fonts are all inside the app. Nothing is fetched while you study.
I think an answer is wrong.
Please email the bank name, the question number and what you believe the correct answer is. Corrections are taken seriously and shipped in the next update.
