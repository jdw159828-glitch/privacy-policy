Privacy Policy — TextLab

Effective 27 September 2026

TextLab collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party code of any kind in the app. This page explains what that means concretely.

What we collect

Nothing. TextLab has no server. We cannot see your documents, your flashcards, your settings, or how you use the app, because none of it is ever sent anywhere.

Where your data lives

Everything stays in the app's own storage on your device:

WhatWhereNotes
DocumentsDocuments/*.md Plain Markdown files, visible in the Files app under TextLab, so you can read, copy or back them up with any other tool.
Flashcard decksApplication Support/decks.jsonA single JSON file.
PreferencesSystem user defaultsOnly your editor font size and chosen theme.

Your data is included in an iCloud or iTunes device backup if you have backups turned on, because that is how iOS backs up any app. That backup is between you and Apple; TextLab is not involved.

Network access

TextLab makes no network requests. The app contains no networking code at all.

The one exception is Apple's own App Store framework, used when you buy, restore, or manage a Pro subscription. That communication is between your device and Apple. We never see your name, your Apple Account, your payment method, or any part of the transaction beyond whether a subscription is currently active on the device.

Text processing

  • Spell checking uses the same on-device dictionary as the iOS keyboard.
  • Summaries are computed locally with a sentence-ranking algorithm.
  • No text is uploaded, and no AI service is involved.

Diagnostic logs

The app writes a small number of diagnostic lines about subscription events to the system log on your device (for example, whether a purchase succeeded). These stay on the device, are not collected by us, and contain no document content.

Children

TextLab is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the flashcard decks and preferences. Documents you have saved in the Files app remain there until you delete them, which is deliberate — your writing should not disappear because you removed an app.

This page

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which like any web host records requests to its servers. That is outside our control and unrelated to the app itself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new date at the top.

Contact

隐私政策 — TextLab

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 27 日

TextLab 不收集任何数据。没有账号、没有分析统计、没有广告，App 内也不含任何第三方代码。 以下是具体说明。

我们收集什么

什么都不收集。 TextLab 没有服务器。我们看不到你的文档、闪卡、设置， 也不知道你如何使用这个 App —— 因为这些数据从不离开你的设备。

你的数据存在哪里

全部保存在设备上 App 自己的存储空间里：

内容位置说明
文档Documents/*.md 纯 Markdown 文件，可在「文件」App 的 TextLab 下直接查看，用任何其他工具读取、复制或备份。
闪卡牌组Application Support/decks.json单个 JSON 文件。
偏好设置系统用户偏好仅编辑器字号和所选主题两项。

如果你开启了设备备份，这些数据会随 iCloud 或 iTunes 备份一并保存 —— 这是 iOS 对所有 App 的统一行为。该备份是你与 Apple 之间的事，与 TextLab 无关。

网络访问

TextLab 不发起任何网络请求，代码中不含任何网络调用。

唯一的例外是 Apple 自己的 App Store 框架：当你购买、恢复或管理 Pro 订阅时会用到它。 这一通信发生在你的设备与 Apple 之间。我们看不到你的姓名、Apple 账户、支付方式，以及交易的 任何细节 —— 只能知道本机当前是否有生效的订阅。

文本处理

  • 拼写检查使用与 iOS 键盘相同的本机词典。
  • 摘要由本地的句子排序算法计算得出。
  • 不上传任何文字，也不涉及任何 AI 服务。

诊断日志

App 会向设备的系统日志写入少量与订阅相关的诊断信息（例如购买是否成功）。这些内容留在 设备本地，我们不会收集，且不含任何文档内容。

儿童

TextLab 分级为 4+，不向任何人（包括儿童）收集数据。

删除你的数据

删除 App 会一并删除闪卡牌组和偏好设置。你保存在「文件」App 中的文档会保留 —— 这是有意为之：不该因为卸载一个 App 就丢失自己写的东西。

关于本页面

本页面托管于 GitHub Pages。与任何网站主机一样，它会记录对其服务器的访问请求。 这不在我们的控制范围内，也与 App 本身无关。

政策变更

若本政策发生变更，新版本会发布在同一地址，并在顶部标注新的日期。
