Common questions

Are these real exam questions?
No, and the app does not claim to be. Every question and explanation was written for this app. They cover indications and contraindications, the handpiece, cups and abrasives, tooth surfaces and deposits, the polishing sequence, infection control, and the supervision rules that decide who may polish. They are not retired examination items, and the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any dental board, certification programme or examination provider.
Why does a tooth go back to unpolished when I miss one question?
Because a tooth you skipped a face on is not a polished tooth. The questions are dealt out across 32 teeth, and a tooth is only finished when every question on it is clean. Partial credit per question would tell you the opposite of what the work teaches.
What does "around the arch" resume on?
The first tooth that is not finished — not the first question you have not seen. The unit of progress is the tooth, so the starting point is a tooth too.
Why do questions mention a state licence like RDA?
Because the scope rules are written around licence titles, and a question about who may polish has to be able to name one. Scope of practice is set by each state's dental board, it changes, and the questions covering it are practice material rather than legal advice. Check your own state's current rules before you work on a patient.
How much is free?
Thirty questions on your first day and twelve a day after that, across all four sets, with no account and no sign-in. A subscription removes the daily limit.
How do I cancel?
Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. The app cannot cancel a subscription for you — Apple does not allow it. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
I subscribed but the app still shows a daily limit
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore. If you subscribed with a different Apple Account, sign in to that account first. If it still does not appear, email the address above and include the Apple Account email you paid with.
Did I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move between devices.
I think a question is wrong
Please send the set name, the tooth number shown above the question, and what you think the answer should be. Questions get corrected in the next update.
