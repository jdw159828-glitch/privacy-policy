Common questions

Does one purchase cover all three charts?
Yes. One payment of $5.99 unlocks all three charts. The 12 free questions a day (30 on your first day) are also shared across all three, and each chart keeps its own progress, so working one does not disturb the others.
What is included?
Colorado Boating (214 questions on Colorado law, lakes and registration), Alabama Boating (202 on Alabama law, rivers and certification), and Boating Safety Basics (201 on navigation rules, required equipment and emergencies).
What does the unlock open?
It removes the daily limit and opens the timed voyage and read-through mode (answers and explanations laid open, unscored). The everyday practice modes work on the free tier, within the daily allowance.
Where is my progress stored?
On your device only. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
I paid but the app still shows the free limit.
Open the unlock page and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.
Is there anything to cancel?
No. This is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It does not renew and nothing is charged again. The unlock is tied to your Apple Account, so it comes back on any device signed in to that account — use Restore purchases.
Does it work without a network?
Yes. The questions, explanations and fonts are all inside the app. Nothing is fetched while you study.
I think an answer is wrong.
Please email the chart name, the question number and what you believe the correct answer is. Corrections are taken seriously and shipped in the next update.
Will this get me a boater education card?
No. Most states issue that card only through a course they have approved, and this app is not such a course. It is practice material for the exam, not the course itself.
Is my state covered?
Two of the three charts are state law — Colorado and Alabama. The third covers the general navigation and safety material that applies wherever you are.
