Common questions

Are these real exam questions?
No, and the app does not claim to be. Every question and explanation was written for this app. They cover charting and radiography, instrument processing and sterilization, terminology and anatomy, tooth numbering and surfaces, chairside assisting and evacuation, jurisprudence, and expanded restorative functions. They are not retired examination items, and the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any dental board, certification body, school or examination.
Why did the room get longer after I got one wrong?
Because a mistake in the operatory costs work, not points. A wrong step adds one more task to that turnover — drawn from the same phase you missed — and the room is not handed over until it is done. A room carries at most six added tasks.
What are the four phases?
Break down and disinfect, set up the tray, seat and drape, assist and hand off. The twelve steps of a room are dealt out three to a phase, and the bars at the top of the screen show how each phase is going, including anything your mistakes added to it.
Can I practise without the room rule?
Yes. "Mixed steps" is single questions with no room and no added tasks, and the catch-up list works the same way. The room rule applies to "Next operatory" and "One operatory".
Is the jurisprudence set legal advice?
No. Scope of practice, supervision levels and registration requirements are set by each state's dental board and they change. Those questions are practice material; check your own state's current rules.
How much is free?
Twenty-eight steps on your first day and twelve a day after that, across all seven sets, with no account and no sign-in. A subscription removes the daily limit.
How do I cancel?
Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. The app cannot cancel a subscription for you — Apple does not allow it. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
I subscribed but the app still shows a daily limit
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore. If you subscribed with a different Apple Account, sign in to that account first. If it still does not appear, email the address above and include the Apple Account email you paid with.
Did I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move between devices.
I think a question is wrong
Please send the set name, the room and step number shown above the question, and what you think the answer should be. Questions get corrected in the next update.
