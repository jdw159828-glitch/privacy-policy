Common questions

Does one purchase cover all three books?
Yes. One payment of $7.99 unlocks all three books. The 12 free questions a day (30 on your first day) are also shared across all three, and each book keeps its own progress, so working one does not disturb the others.
What is included?
California Notary (233 entries on California law, the journal, the seal and fees), Connecticut Notary (210 on Connecticut law, notarial acts and appointment), and Colorado Notary (188 on Colorado law under RULONA, including remote acts).
What does the unlock open?
It removes the daily limit and opens the scored commission exam and read-through mode (answers and explanations laid open, unscored). The everyday practice modes work on the free tier, within the daily allowance.
Where is my progress stored?
On your device only. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
I paid but the app still shows the free limit.
Open the unlock page and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.
Is there anything to cancel?
No. This is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It does not renew and nothing is charged again. The unlock is tied to your Apple Account, so it comes back on any device signed in to that account — use Restore purchases.
Does it work without a network?
Yes. The questions, explanations and fonts are all inside the app. Nothing is fetched while you study.
I think an answer is wrong.
Please email the book name, the question number and what you believe the correct answer is. Corrections are taken seriously and shipped in the next update.
Is my state covered?
Only California, Connecticut and Colorado. Notary law is state law and these books do not mix the three. If you are commissioned elsewhere, this app will not match your exam.
