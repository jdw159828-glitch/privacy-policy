Common questions

Are these real exam questions?
No, and the app does not claim to be. Every question and explanation was written for this app. They cover mammography positioning and quality control, radiopharmaceuticals and gamma cameras, vascular access and contrast, CT protocols and artifacts, and bone densitometry sites and precision. They are not retired examination items, and the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any registry, certifying body or examination.
Why did my level go down?
Because a study with three or more misses is a repeated study, and a repeated study drops you back one level. Two clean studies — no misses at all — move you up one. One or two misses is an accepted study: diagnostic, but nothing moves. The ladder is Observing, Assisting, Scanning, Signed off, and it runs both ways.
Is "Signed off" a credential?
No. It is a label inside this app for a modality whose studies you have been running clean. It is not issued by anyone, it does not travel with you, and it has nothing to do with any registry's requirements.
Are the console readings real protocols?
They are realistic values for each modality, shown so that the console you are sitting at looks like that machine and not a generic quiz. They are not a protocol to work to, and a miss marking a reading "off protocol" is a scoring cue, not a machine state. Your department's protocols are the ones that count.
How much is free?
Thirty-two images on your first day and twelve a day after that, across all five consoles, with no account and no sign-in. A subscription removes the daily limit.
How do I cancel?
Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. The app cannot cancel a subscription for you — Apple does not allow it. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
I subscribed but the app still shows a daily limit
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore. If you subscribed with a different Apple Account, sign in to that account first. If it still does not appear, email the address above and include the Apple Account email you paid with.
Did I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move between devices.
I think a question is wrong
Please send the console name, the study and image number shown above the question, and what you think the answer should be. Questions get corrected in the next update.

Privacy
