Common questions

Are these real exam questions?
No, and the app does not claim to be. Every question and explanation was written for this app. They cover pharmacy calculations, pharmacology, sig codes, controlled substances and records, compounding, patient contact, and immunization support. They are not retired examination items, and the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any certification board, training provider or examination.
Why did a question I got wrong come straight back?
Because that is what happens at the counter. A script the pharmacist hands back does not wait for another day — it is re-queued five scripts later in the same shift and marked as a re-check. Clear it and it is filled. Miss it twice and it goes to the hold bin, which you can work on its own whenever you like.
What is "first pass"?
How often you were right the first time you saw a script, before any re-check. It is the number the app leads with, because filling right the first time is the thing a bench is actually measured on. Your overall fill count includes the ones you cleared on the second look; first pass does not.
Is this clinical advice?
No. It is practice material. Dispense to your own pharmacy's procedures, the current law in your state, and the direction of the supervising pharmacist. Drug names, doses and schedules change; nothing here replaces the current reference you work from.
How much is free?
Twenty-six scripts on your first day and ten a day after that, across all three sets, with no account and no sign-in. A subscription removes the daily limit.
How do I cancel?
Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. The app cannot cancel a subscription for you — Apple does not allow it. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
I subscribed but the app still shows a daily limit
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore. If you subscribed with a different Apple Account, sign in to that account first. If it still does not appear, email the address above and include the Apple Account email you paid with.
Did I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move between devices.
I think a question is wrong
Please send the set name, the Rx number shown at the top of the label, and what you think the answer should be. Questions get corrected in the next update.
