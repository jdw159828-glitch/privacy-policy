Common questions

Are these real exam questions?
No, and the app does not claim to be. Every item and every explanation was written for this app. They cover human development, assessment and diagnosis, risk and safety, intervention planning, and professional ethics — the subject matter social work licensing exams draw on. They are not retired examination items, and the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any licensing board, examination provider or professional association.
Which level should I use?
There are three case files — bachelor, master and clinical. They are separate files with separate progress, so you can work only the one that matches the licence you are sitting for, or work all three. A subscription covers all three.
What does "filed" and "supervision" mean?
An item you answer correctly is filed into one of the six assessment sections. An item you get wrong goes to the supervision list, and stays there until you answer it correctly. The supervision list is the fastest way to study: it is exactly the material you have already shown you do not know.
How much is free?
Thirty items on your first day and ten items a day after that, across all three case files, with no account and no sign-in. A subscription removes the daily limit.
How do I cancel?
Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. The app cannot cancel a subscription for you — Apple does not allow it. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
I subscribed but the app still shows a daily limit
Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore. If you subscribed with a different Apple Account, sign in to that account first. If it still does not appear, email the address above and include the Apple Account email you paid with.
Did I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move between devices.
I think an item is wrong
Please send the case file name, the item number shown above the question, and what you think the answer should be. Items get corrected in the next update.

Privacy
