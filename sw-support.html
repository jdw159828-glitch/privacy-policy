Common questions

What is in the app?
Three tracks — social work, notary public and funeral service — in nine sets, 1,962 practice items, each with a written explanation. One subscription covers every track; nothing is sold separately and there is no companion app.
Are these real exam questions?
No, and the app does not claim to be. Every item and explanation was written for this app. They are not retired examination questions, and the app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any licensing board, state agency, school or examination provider.
What does "held for review" mean?
It is what happens to an item you got wrong. The item is not lost and it is not a lost point — it is still open. A section of twenty-five items closes only when all twenty-five are filed, so one held item keeps that whole section open.
How do I close a section?
Open "Held for review". It lists exactly the items keeping sections open. Answer one correctly and it is filed; when the last one in a section is filed, the section closes and its square turns green.
Why score it that way instead of a percentage?
Because a case record, a notary journal and a funeral file are not judged on an average. They are judged on whether anything is still outstanding. A percentage lets a gap hide behind a good score; a held item does not go away until you deal with it.
Why are these three in the same app?
Because all three are state-licensed callings whose work is the record. The social worker's case file, the notary's journal and the funeral director's file are the same shape of obligation: every required entry has to be there. Three tracks under one subscription costs less than three apps.
Can I practise without the section rule?
Yes. "Mixed items" is every item shuffled, with nothing to close. The section rule applies to "Work the file" and "One section".
What is a section?
Twenty-five items. The grid at the bottom of a set shows each one: a green border means it is closed, an amber border means something in it is held, and the bar underneath shows how much of it is filed.
Why can't I start a board exam?
The timed exams are part of a subscription. Practice is not — every one of the 1,962 items is reachable for free, a few a day. We made it this way because a 50-item timed exam cannot be done a few items at a time: you would be stopped halfway with the clock running, which is worse than not starting it. The pass mark is 70%.
Are the notary sets specific to my state?
Yes — they are California, Connecticut and Colorado, and each is labelled. Notary law is set state by state and it changes, so check your own state's current rules before you rely on anything here. The same goes for funeral law and the general price list.
Is any of this legal advice or a work procedure?
No. The file, the held item and the closed section are a scoring device invented for this app. Follow your board's rules, your state's statutes and your employer's procedures.
How much is free?
Twenty-six items on your first day — enough to close a whole section — and ten a day after that, across all nine sets, with no account and no sign-in. A subscription removes the daily limit and opens the timed exams.
How do I cancel?
Open the Settings app on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. The app cannot cancel a subscription for you — Apple does not allow it. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period runs to its end.
I subscribed but the app still shows a daily limit
Open Settings inside the app (the gear, top right) and tap Restore. If you subscribed with a different Apple Account, sign in to that account first. If it still does not appear, email the address above and include the Apple Account email you paid with.
Did I lose my progress?
Progress is stored on the device only — there is no account and nothing is uploaded. It survives app updates, but deleting the app deletes it, and it does not move between devices.
I think an item is wrong
Please send the track, the set name, and the item number shown above the question, along with what you think the answer should be. Items get corrected in the next update.
