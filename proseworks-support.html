Common questions

Does Proseworks work offline?

Entirely. Writing, proofreading, summarising and flashcards all run on your device. The app makes no network requests at all. The only thing that needs a connection is buying or restoring a subscription, which goes through Apple.

Where are my documents kept, and can I get them out?

Every document is a plain .md file in the Files app under Proseworks. You can open, copy, email or back them up with any other tool, and they stay there even if you delete the app.

What does Pro unlock?

Unlimited documents and decks, all seven proofreading checks instead of two, summaries of any length, and the three extra editor themes. The free tier keeps 3 documents, 1 deck of up to 40 cards, and summarises up to 1,500 characters.

What happens to my work if my subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted. Every document and card you already have stays, and stays editable. You simply cannot create new ones past the free limits until you subscribe again, and the editor returns to the Paper theme.

I paid but Pro is not active

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. If that does not help, check that the device is signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. Email us if it still will not activate.

How do I change or cancel my subscription?

In the app, open Settings › Change or cancel plan. Apple's own sheet handles it. Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by this app, so you can also do it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions on your device.

Switching from yearly to monthly is a downgrade, so Apple applies it at the end of the period you already paid for — the app shows you the date it will take effect.

The proofreader flagged something that is not wrong

Spelling comes from the system dictionary, but the style checks — long sentences, filler words, passive voice — are rules of thumb, not grammar. They are meant as prompts, not verdicts. Turn any check off with the filter in the top left of the Proofread screen.

Spell check is not finding anything

It uses the dictionary for the language iOS detects in your text. If that language pack is not installed on the device, there is nothing to check against. Adding the keyboard for that language in iOS Settings installs it.

Why does the summary not rewrite my text?

By design. Proseworks picks out the most central sentences of what you wrote rather than generating new wording. It is not an AI service, and the trade-off is deliberate: a summary can never invent something you did not write.

How does flashcard review decide when a card comes back?

SM-2, the spaced-repetition algorithm behind SuperMemo and Anki's default. Each grading button shows the interval it will set before you tap it. Grade yourself honestly — being generous only means forgetting the word later.

Does my subscription work on my other devices?

Yes, on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Your documents and cards do not sync between devices, though — they are stored locally, and there is no server to sync them through.

About the tools

The proofreader and summariser are deterministic, on-device algorithms, not language models. They will not match a human editor, and they are not intended to. Proseworks is a writing tool, not a proofreading service.
